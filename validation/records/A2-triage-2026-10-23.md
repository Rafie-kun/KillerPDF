## Render completeness and the batch option

Since 3f45603 the batch renderer refuses pages whose diagnostics block publication. A first run at cb4276a without an option therefore produced no image for 29 conformance and 56 PDF.js pages that rendered on 2026-10-09. Gate runs now pass `--allow-incomplete` (d95d21c and 8065c4e), which writes those pages and keeps their diagnostics in the log's Detail column. Without the option, the refusal is unchanged.
