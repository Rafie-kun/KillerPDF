## Render completeness and the batch option

Since 3f45603 the batch renderer refuses pages whose diagnostics block publication. A first run at cb4276a without an option therefore produced no image for 29 conformance and 56 PDF.js pages that rendered on 2026-10-09. Of those 85 pages, 59 report omitted content and 26 report only recoveries or substitutions, such as an ignored ToUnicode map, a clamped miter limit, recovered Type 1 lengths, an ICC alternate color space, a font replaced by Helvetica, or a regenerated form-field appearance.

Gate runs now pass `--allow-incomplete` (d95d21c and 8065c4e), which writes those pages and keeps their diagnostics in the log's Detail column. Without the option, the refusal is unchanged. With it, conformance renders 634 pages with 15 skips, the accepted denominator, and PDF.js renders 1,100 pages, two more than on 2026-10-09. Including the 85 pages added no outlier.
